குட்டி குரல் (Kutti Kural) Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026

In short: Kutti Kural is an app for young children learning the Tamil alphabet. It has no accounts, asks for no personal information, and keeps your child's progress on the phone. Banner ads come from Google AdMob with children's settings switched on: no personalised ads and no advertising ID.

This policy explains what information the Kutti Kural Android app ("the app") handles. The app is made by KalSel.Prod ("we"). It is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-in, no account, no chat, no camera and no microphone use, and it does not ask for a name, email address, phone number, photo or location. We do not run our own servers and receive no data from the app.

Information stored on the device

The app saves a few settings on the phone itself so it can pick up where your child left off:

  • which letters have been traced and which words have been built (the sticker book)
  • the number of balloons popped and the best quiz score
  • whether music is on or off
  • whether the ad-free version has been bought

This information never leaves the device through the app. If Android backup is switched on for the phone's Google account, Android may include it in that backup so it can be restored on a new phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising

The free version shows a small banner ad on menu and letter-list screens, never while a child is tracing or playing a game. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. We have set every ad request as directed at children:

  • the request is marked for child users (age-restricted treatment)
  • personalised ads are switched off, so ads are not based on interests or past activity
  • ads are limited to content rated G (suitable for general audiences)
  • the app does not access the device's advertising ID; that permission is removed from the app

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect limited technical information such as the device model, Android version, IP address, and whether an ad was shown or tapped, and may send crash reports related to ads. Google uses this as described in its policies for child-directed requests. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and AdMob and child-directed treatment.

In-app purchase

A grown-up can buy a one-time "Remove ads" upgrade. Before the purchase screen opens, the app asks a multiplication question that young children cannot easily answer. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play; we never see card details or the buyer's identity. The app only checks with Google Play whether the upgrade has been bought. See the Google Payments privacy notice.

Speech

The app reads Tamil letters and words aloud using the text-to-speech engine installed on the phone. It only sends the app's own letters and words to that engine. It does not record or listen to your child.

Children's privacy

The app is made for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app gives children no way to share information with us or with others. The app contains no web links, no social features and no purchases that a child can complete without a grown-up.

Your choices

  • Buy the ad-free upgrade to remove all ads.
  • Clear the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the app, to delete its saved progress.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
